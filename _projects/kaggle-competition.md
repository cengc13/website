### **Bengali.AI Handwritten Grapheme Classification**

<div class="row align-items-center mt-3">
    <div class="col-md-4 mb-3 mb-md-0">
        {% include figure.html zoomable="true" path="assets/img/projects/kaggle-competitions/kaggle-bengali-desc.png" title="Bengali.AI Handwritten Grapheme Classification" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}
    </div>
    <div class="col-md-8">
        <p><strong>Silver medal (leader of team "Zzz..."), ranked 90th of 2059 teams</strong> · Computer vision</p>
        <p>Multi-class image classification of handwritten Bengali graphemes. Bengali has 49 letters and 18 diacritics, which combine into roughly 13,000 grapheme variants. Each image had to be classified into three parts: the grapheme root, the vowel diacritic and the consonant diacritic.</p>
        <p>Blogs: <a href="{{ '/kaggle/2020/02/18/kaggle-bengali-start.html' | relative_url }}">start</a> · <a href="{{ '/kaggle/2020/02/25/kaggle-bengali-midway.html' | relative_url }}">midway</a> · <a href="{{ '/kaggle/2020/03/03/kaggle-bengali-final.html' | relative_url }}">final</a> · <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/c/bengaliai-cv19">competition page</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00170">dataset paper</a></p>
