Machine learning competitions on Kaggle

Kaggle Competitions Master with 1 gold and 2 silver medals in computer vision, NLP and forecasting

In 2020 I took part in four Kaggle machine learning competitions, both on my own and as a team leader, and wrote a three-part blog series (start, midway, final) for each.

  • 1 gold and 2 silver medals
  • Kaggle Competitions Master in about six months
  • Highest global rank of 270 / 144,681

Jigsaw Multilingual Toxic Comment Classification

Gold medal (solo), ranked 5th of 1621 teams · Natural language processing

Zero- and few-shot multilingual toxicity classification: models were trained mostly on English comments and tested on Wikipedia talk-page comments in several other languages, using Kaggle's TPUs and multilingual transformers.

Blogs: start · midway · final · competition page

Bengali.AI Handwritten Grapheme Classification

Silver medal (leader of team "Zzz..."), ranked 90th of 2059 teams · Computer vision

Multi-class image classification of handwritten Bengali graphemes. Bengali has 49 letters and 18 diacritics, which combine into roughly 13,000 grapheme variants. Each image had to be classified into three parts: the grapheme root, the vowel diacritic and the consonant diacritic.

Blogs: start · midway · final · competition page · dataset paper

Tweet Sentiment Extraction

Silver medal (solo), ranked 90th of 2225 teams · Natural language processing

Span extraction: given a tweet and its sentiment label, find the word or phrase that supports the sentiment. For example, in "My ridiculous dog is amazing." [positive], the answer is "amazing".

Blogs: start · midway · final · competition page

M5 Forecasting - Accuracy

Ranked 645th of 5558 teams · Time-series forecasting

Hierarchical forecasting of daily unit sales for Walmart products over the next 28 days. The data covered stores in California, Texas and Wisconsin, with explanatory variables such as price, promotions and special events.

Blogs: start · midway · final · competition page
